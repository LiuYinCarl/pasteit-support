PasteIt

A fast, private clipboard history manager for macOS.

Version 2.1.0 macOS 26 or later Mac App Store

About

PasteIt keeps a history of the text and images you copy, so nothing you copy is ever lost. Entries live in one place, can be collected automatically while you work (optional, off by default), are searchable in real time, and are one double-click away from being copied back.

Everything is stored locally on your Mac. PasteIt works entirely offline — it has no analytics, no ads, and never uploads your clipboard.

Features

Clipboard entries

Keep text and images as reusable entries. Pin favorites, rename, and reorder by drag & drop.

Auto-collect

Optionally capture text, images, and file paths copied in other apps. Apps such as password managers can be excluded.

Timeline

Every copied item becomes a card. Double-click to copy it back; deleted cards are recoverable.

Text editor

Edit captured text with auto-save and word count, copy the whole text, or reveal its folder in Finder.

Images

Paste or drag images in, browse downscaled thumbnails, and preview full size with pinch-to-zoom.

Recycle Bin

Deleted items are kept for restoration. Storage limits clear out the oldest content automatically.

Search

Instantly filter entries by name or content — performed in the background, never blocking the UI.

Menu bar & hotkey

Resides in the menu bar, wakes with a customizable global hotkey, and can launch at login.

Light & dark · 6 languages

Follows the system appearance, with English, Chinese, French, Russian, Spanish, and Arabic built in.

Frequently asked questions

Where is my data stored?

On your Mac only, in ~/Library/Application Support/PasteIt/. Nothing is uploaded to any server. If you delete the app, this folder (and your history) remains until you remove it yourself.

Does PasteIt connect to the internet?

No. PasteIt works entirely offline: no analytics, no telemetry, no ads, and no third-party SDKs. The Mac App Store version is sandboxed and has no network entitlements at all.

How do I stop PasteIt from recording certain apps (for example, my password manager)?

Auto-collect is off by default and only runs while it is enabled. For extra control, open Settings → Privacy Exclusion and add apps by name or bundle identifier — anything copied from those apps is never recorded.

How do I delete everything PasteIt has collected?

Delete items in the app (they go to the Recycle Bin, where you can remove them permanently), or quit PasteIt and delete the folder ~/Library/Application Support/PasteIt/ to erase all data at once.

Can I bring my history from the Windows version of PasteIt?

Yes. The data layout is identical. Copy your old Data/ and Recycle/ folders into ~/Library/Application Support/PasteIt/ and your entries appear seamlessly.

Which macOS versions are supported?

macOS 26 or later.

The global hotkey doesn't respond.

Another app may already use the same key combination. Choose a different combination in Settings → Hotkey. Launching PasteIt again also wakes the running instance if the window is hidden.

Auto-collect stopped capturing items.

Check that Auto-collect is enabled in the menu bar menu or Settings, and that the source app is not on the Privacy Exclusion list. Duplicate content is also skipped: identical text is collected once in a row, and the most recent images are de-duplicated by content.
