Privacy Policy

PasteIt for macOS

Effective date: September 17, 2026 · Applies to: PasteIt (Mac App Store and direct distribution)

The short version

PasteIt does not collect, transmit, or share any of your data. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking, and no third-party services. Everything the app stores — including your clipboard history — stays on your Mac, in files you can see and delete.

1. Information we collect

None. PasteIt does not collect any information from you or about you. This matches the “Data Not Collected” disclosure on its App Store privacy label.

2. Clipboard data

PasteIt is a clipboard history manager, so its core purpose is to store the content you copy. How this works:

  • Local only. Captured text, images, and file paths are written to ~/Library/Application Support/PasteIt/ on your Mac. They are never uploaded or transmitted anywhere.
  • Auto-collect is opt-in. Background clipboard collection is switched off by default and starts only if you turn it on.
  • Exclusions. You can exclude specific applications (for example, password managers) by name or bundle identifier. Content copied from excluded apps is never recorded.
  • You stay in control. You can delete individual items at any time, empty the Recycle Bin, or erase everything by removing the data folder.

3. Where your data is stored

All app data is stored locally on your Mac under ~/Library/Application Support/PasteIt/ (entries, timeline, images, Recycle Bin, and settings). Deleting the PasteIt app does not delete this folder; to remove all data permanently, delete the folder itself. Preferences are stored on device using the standard macOS defaults system.

4. Permissions and system APIs

The Mac App Store version of PasteIt runs inside the macOS App Sandbox and requests only:

  • User-selected file access — to open or save files and folders you explicitly choose.
  • File timestamps — used on device only, to order and sort your entries.
  • UserDefaults — used on device only, to remember your preferences such as theme, language, and hotkey.

None of these transmit data off your device.

5. Third-party services

PasteIt contains no third-party SDKs, analytics frameworks, advertising networks, or crash-reporting services, and communicates with no servers.

6. Purchases

If you obtain PasteIt from the Mac App Store, the purchase is processed by Apple under Apple's own privacy policy. The developer never receives your payment details or App Store account information.

7. Children's privacy

PasteIt is a general-purpose utility and does not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

8. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page with a new effective date. Material changes will also be noted in the app's release notes.

9. Contact

Questions about this policy? Open an issue on GitHub or visit the support page.
